Welcome to TUChess!

TUChess is a free and open club for people at the Technical University extended to friends, neighbours, students, teachers or simply anyone. Our goal is to create and host safe, friendly and fun chess meet-ups and tournaments. Take a look at our events to see what's planned and coming up! If you are new to chess, don't worry! We welcome players of all skill levels and are happy to help you learn and improve. Anyone is welcome to come and play. You can bring your own chess set or use one of ours. Becoming and official member is completely optional and free of charge, but not required to participate in our events. If you want to become a member, perks include access to the leaderboard, voting rights for the club and more. Check it out on the players page! If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to me via email at my campus mail.
